# The refusals that have no entry in tests/mutation/catalog.txt, and why. One per
# line, three fields separated by TAB:
#
#   file <TAB> a text that lies on the lines of the refusal <TAB> the reason
# The text lies on the refusal itself, its condition, or the header of the block that
# holds it. A text of `*` exempts every refusal of the file. A reason says what was
# measured: that the refusal cannot be reached, that its mutation is equivalent, or
# that only the real build of the package exercises it, named.
# scripts/quality/check-mutation-coverage.sh fails when a refusal it reads has neither
# an entry nor a line here, and when a line here has outlived its premise: its text is
# gone, it reaches no refusal, or an entry now covers what it exempts. A line is
# retired by writing the entry, or the test that makes an entry possible, and deleting
# the line in the same change.
